# ProofForge

## Evidence Assurance for AI and Software Decisions

ProofForge is a developer-facing evidence and assurance product family designed to help teams inspect claims, evidence, and machine-generated decisions with a clearer record of what supports the result.

### Public product family

- **Evidence Claim Check Report** — reviews a claim against supplied evidence and produces a structured evidence-focused result.
- **Evidence Assurance Report** — deeper evidence organization and assurance output for higher-confidence review.
- **Agent Decision Audit** — examines an AI/agent decision and the evidence trail supporting that decision.

### Best fit

- AI product teams
- SaaS founders
- Agent developers
- Software teams that need stronger evidence trails
- Teams preparing internal or external review material

### Positioning

ProofForge is not presented as a replacement for legal, regulatory, or professional judgment. It is a technical evidence-assurance layer designed to make review more structured, inspectable, and useful.
