## Premium / Enterprise

A.C.E. is Black Knight Technology's premium commercial assurance and governed autonomous-commerce infrastructure platform.

It is positioned for advanced operators that need more than a single-purpose developer utility: stronger decision control, commercial-readiness checks, machine-executable workflows, evidence-backed release decisions, and governed automation across complex product or service operations.

### Two ways A.C.E. is exposed

A.C.E. has both **bounded machine-service surfaces** and a separate **enterprise licensing path**.

- **Machine-service surfaces** expose narrowly scoped, payment-gated capabilities for qualified technical workflows. Access to a bounded service does **not** grant a software license, source-code access, deployment rights, or enterprise runtime authority.
- **Enterprise licensing** is the premium path for OEM, advanced internal-use, evaluation, or governed integration engagements. It begins through controlled qualification and proceeds only through approved agreement, payment-verification, provisioning, and evidence gates.

This distinction keeps low-scope API execution separate from ownership or licensing rights.
