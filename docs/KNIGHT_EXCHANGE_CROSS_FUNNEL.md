# Knight Exchange ↔ Black Knight Technology Cross-Funnel

The two public markets solve different parts of the same commercial problem and should refer qualified users to one another without merging their private systems.

## Knight Exchange → Black Knight Technology
When a buyer or provider needs deeper technical assurance, Knight Exchange may refer to Black Knight Technology for:
- API/release preflight;
- evidence verification;
- software readiness;
- agent/workflow assurance;
- governed commerce evaluation.

Public message: **Need deeper verification or technical assurance? Use Black Knight Technology.**

## Black Knight Technology → Knight Exchange
When a Black Knight Technology buyer has a clearly scoped task but needs someone to perform the work, route them to Knight Exchange.

Public message: **Need a qualified provider to perform the work? Post it on Knight Exchange.**

Knight Exchange: https://github.com/kingdt1991-netizen/Knight-Exchange

## Boundary
The cross-funnel passes only public-safe context and user-selected links. It does not expose private Backyard logic, GPR/ZEI internals, credentials, payment secrets, customer-confidential data, private source, or Crown IP.

## Attribution
Use `source=knight-exchange` for traffic referred into Black Knight Technology where the destination supports campaign parameters. Knight Exchange should likewise use a Black Knight Technology source identifier for referrals in the other direction.

## Success
A cross-funnel conversion is counted only when an attributable downstream action occurs. A link impression alone is not a sale.
