# Evidence Assurance

For teams that need to determine whether a claim, report, evidence package, provenance trail, or agent decision is actually supported.

## Outcome
Turn assertions into an evidence-backed review with clear findings and a reproducible trail appropriate to the selected service.

## Stack
- **ProofForge** — primary evidence and decision assurance.
- **RightsGate** — optional readiness/rights context when the evidence relates to commercialization or software readiness.
- **TaskForge** — optional bounded execution when new technical evidence must be generated.

## Best fit
- evidence packages and technical claims;
- AI-agent decision trails;
- diligence and readiness evidence;
- provenance questions;
- higher-stakes completion claims.

## What the buyer receives
The deliverable associated with the selected ProofForge product, ranging from bounded machine results to human-reviewed evidence reports. Additional RightsGate or TaskForge work is separately scoped when needed.

## Pricing and payment
ProofForge currently offers bounded x402 services plus fixed-price human-reviewed reports at published prices. Optional supporting products retain their current public prices. No unpublished package price is claimed.
