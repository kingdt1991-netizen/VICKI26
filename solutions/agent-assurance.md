# Agent Assurance

For teams operating AI agents, MCP tools, automated workflows, or machine-to-machine services that need bounded execution plus evidence of what actually happened.

## Outcome
Move from “the agent says it worked” to an evidence-backed execution and assurance trail.

## Stack
- **TaskForge Agent Assurance** — bounded structured execution and machine-readable results.
- **ProofForge** — evidence and decision assurance.
- **QuickScan** — optional preflight for configuration/release artifacts.

## Best fit
- AI-agent operators;
- MCP and tool developers;
- automated workflow teams;
- machine-service buyers who need evidence-backed completion.

## What the buyer receives
The selected TaskForge execution result plus the applicable completion evidence. ProofForge can add a deeper evidence/decision review when the buyer needs more than the bounded machine result.

## Pricing and payment
TaskForge services currently use direct USDC on Base via x402 at the published catalog price. ProofForge may use Stripe for human-reviewed reports or x402 for bounded machine services. No new bundle price is implied until published.
