# Commerce Readiness

For enterprises and advanced operators evaluating governed autonomous-commerce infrastructure rather than a single bounded check.

## Outcome
Determine whether A.C.E. and the Black Knight assurance stack fit the buyer's governed-commerce requirements before licensing or a larger implementation engagement.

## Stack
- **A.C.E. — Autonomous Commerce Engine** — governed decision and commerce infrastructure.
- **ProofForge** — evidence/decision assurance where applicable.
- **RightsGate** — readiness/rights review where applicable.
- **TaskForge / QuickScan** — bounded technical checks where appropriate.

## Best fit
- enterprise/OEM evaluation;
- governed agent-commerce systems;
- teams that need approval boundaries and evidence-backed decisions;
- buyers evaluating a controlled commercial deployment.

## What the buyer receives
A fit/evaluation path leading to a separately scoped enterprise engagement when appropriate. Viewing public material or using a bounded machine service does not grant A.C.E. production, source, deployment, redistribution, OEM, or licensing rights.

## Pricing and payment
The public A.C.E. evaluation is free. Enterprise licensing and custom engagements are separately qualified and priced. Supporting bounded or report products retain their published prices and payment rails.
