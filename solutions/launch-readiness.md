# Launch Readiness

For founders and teams preparing software—especially AI-built software—for customers, licensing, diligence, or production launch.

## Outcome
Reduce avoidable launch risk by combining preflight scanning, commercial-readiness review, and evidence assurance.

## Stack
- **QuickScan** — configuration and release-artifact preflight.
- **RightsGate** — software/commercial readiness review.
- **ProofForge** — evidence and claim verification where needed.

## Best fit
- pre-launch SaaS and AI products;
- teams preparing customer or investor diligence;
- software being commercialized or licensed;
- releases that need a clearer evidence trail before launch.

## What the buyer receives
A scoped readiness result identifying material release risks, commercial-readiness concerns, evidence gaps, and prioritized next actions. Exact deliverables depend on the selected underlying product tier.

## Pricing and payment
Uses the current public prices of the included products. Fixed-price RightsGate and ProofForge reports use Stripe. Bounded machine checks may use USDC on Base via x402. Bundled discounts or new fixed bundle prices are not promised until separately published.
