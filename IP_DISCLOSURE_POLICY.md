# Disclosure Control Policy

Use the least-disclosure level necessary.

## Level 0 — Public summary

May include the problem, audience, business model, high-level benefits, non-confidential screenshots, and verified milestones. Do not include source code, secrets, detailed algorithms, unpublished canon, private datasets, internal prompts, credentials, or enabling technical schematics.

## Level 1 — Controlled evaluation

May be shared only with an identified recipient for a defined purpose. Mark material confidential, record what was shared and when, and provide only the minimum technical detail needed.

## Level 2 — Confidential diligence

Detailed architecture, financial information, provenance evidence, and product demonstrations require a controlled data room and written confidentiality terms appropriate to the relationship.

## Level 3 — Source-level access

Source code, database access, model artifacts, private voice data, secrets, and complete invention disclosures remain owner-controlled. When necessary, use supervised, time-limited, read-only access. Never provide production credentials.

## Required disclosure log

Record the recipient, date/time, purpose, files or links shared, confidentiality status, expiration/revocation date, and owner approval. A public disclosure involving a potentially patentable invention must be escalated immediately because it may affect patent rights.
