# Proof & Activity Publication Policy

Black Knight Technology publishes operational proof only when the underlying event can be supported by production evidence.

## Allowed public metrics

A metric may be shown publicly only when it comes from an approved source and its meaning is unambiguous:

- independently verified paid settlements;
- successful paid fulfillment records;
- active production endpoints confirmed by a current health/payment challenge;
- Proof Receipts tied to completed work;
- completed human-reviewed reports with payment and fulfillment evidence;
- payment rails confirmed operational.

## Not customer activity

The following must never be presented as customers, sales, revenue, or market traction:

- synthetic or sandbox tests;
- owner/operator test calls;
- unpaid x402 challenges;
- bots or crawlers merely requesting a page;
- GitHub stars, watchers, clones, views, or impressions without conversion evidence;
- internal cron/worker activity;
- failed or abandoned checkout sessions;
- unverified marketplace listings;
- outreach messages sent.

## Traffic versus conversion

Traffic is useful only when its source is dependable. The current GitHub integration does not provide Black Knight Technology with a dependable repository page-view/unique-visitor feed, so VICKI26 does not invent or estimate GitHub traffic.

Commercial reporting prioritizes downstream evidence:

**qualified lead → attributed action → checkout/intake → paid/qualified → fulfilled → independently verified settlement → repeat buyer**

## Proof labels

A public badge such as `Live Checkout`, `Payment-Gated Execution`, `Production Tested`, or `Verified Sale` must be backed by evidence appropriate to the claim. A badge does not imply a sales volume, revenue level, or customer count unless those numbers are separately verified.

## Privacy

Public metrics must never expose customer identity, email, wallet signing material, payment credentials, processor secrets, private evidence, private repository paths, Crown IP, or proprietary scoring logic.

## Corrections

If a published metric or proof label becomes stale, ambiguous, or contradicted by stronger evidence, it must be corrected or removed rather than silently preserved.
