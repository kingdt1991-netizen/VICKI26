# Proof of Operation

**Black Knight Technology**

Black Knight Technology uses evidence-backed proof labels to distinguish deployed capability from customer adoption. A badge is added only when the underlying system has produced observable evidence that supports the claim.

## Proof-label definitions

- **Production Tested** — repeatable production-oriented or golden-path tests have executed successfully against the deployed product path.
- **Fail-Closed Verified** — invalid, incomplete, unauthorized, or unsafe execution attempts have been observed stopping instead of bypassing a required control.
- **Live Checkout** — a production-mode customer payment path is active.
- **Production Intake Deployed** — the post-purchase or prospect intake path is deployed and active.
- **Paid Intake Deployed** — the payment-to-intake handoff is deployed and active.
- **Fulfillment Worker Deployed** — a dedicated worker exists for the product's post-intake fulfillment path.
- **Runtime Exercised** — the deployed runtime has processed substantial test or market traffic.
- **Marketplace Field-Tested** — the product has been exposed to live marketplace or external-workflow conditions rather than only isolated local tests.
- **Payment-Gated Execution** — execution is designed to remain behind payment or settlement verification where payment is required.
- **Customer Payment Verified** — an externally settled customer payment has been matched to its on-chain transaction evidence.
- **Customer Delivery Verified** — a paid request completed and the purchased result was returned through the documented delivery path.
- **Enterprise Licensing Ready** — the controlled enterprise licensing path, agreement selection, signing controls, and release gates have passed production acceptance.
- **Field Outreach Tested** — outreach has actually been submitted through a live communications provider; this does not imply delivery, reply, or conversion unless separately verified.

## Evidence policy

Black Knight Technology separates these evidence classes:

1. **Synthetic / regression evidence** — controlled test data and test-only workflows.
2. **Production-acceptance evidence** — deployed system behavior tested against release gates.
3. **Field-operation evidence** — real external systems, marketplaces, communications providers, or live storefronts were involved.
4. **Customer evidence** — independently verified customer payment, fulfillment, delivery, or outcome.

Customer-payment and customer-delivery claims are intentionally withheld unless the underlying evidence supports them.
