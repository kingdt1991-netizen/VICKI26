# Black Knight Technology

**Black Knight Technology** is the public-facing technology business and brand under which the software products in this portfolio are developed, marketed, licensed, and supported.

Products presented through this storefront — including **A.C.E. (Autonomous Commerce Engine), RightsGate, ProofForge, and TaskForge** — are Black Knight Technology products.

Black Knight Technology operates as an assumed name of Black Knight Transportation LLC. That underlying legal entity is used where formal contracting, invoicing, banking, tax, or other legal documentation requires the contracting party to be identified.

## Public brand identity

- Public technology business: **Black Knight Technology**
- Product portfolio: **A.C.E., RightsGate, ProofForge, TaskForge, and other approved Black Knight Technology software**
- Public product ownership/branding: **Black Knight Technology**

## Legal contracting identity

Formal agreements may identify the contracting party as **Black Knight Transportation LLC d/b/a Black Knight Technology** so the public-facing technology brand and the legal contracting entity remain properly connected.

The public storefront does not need to foreground the underlying LLC. Customer-facing product pages should lead with **Black Knight Technology** unless a legal, tax, payment, or contractual document requires the full contracting identity.

Existing service keys, endpoints, deployment identifiers, and payment routing remain unchanged unless separately approved.
