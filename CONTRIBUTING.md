# Contribution and Ownership Policy

This is a proprietary project. Unsolicited contributions are not accepted.

Before any outside contribution is merged, the owner must have a written agreement that addresses:

1. the contributor's identity and authority to contribute;
2. assignment or an expressly approved license covering the contribution;
3. confirmation that the work is original or properly licensed;
4. disclosure of third-party code, assets, datasets, models, voices, samples, and fonts;
5. disclosure of material generated with AI tools, including the tool/model and source inputs where known;
6. permission for commercial use, modification, distribution, sublicensing, and enforcement;
7. continuing confidentiality obligations for non-public material.

A pull request, commit, message, payment, or repository access by itself does not establish an ownership transfer.

Do not include secrets, personal data, customer records, private voice samples, unlicensed media, copied source code, or confidential information belonging to another person or company.
